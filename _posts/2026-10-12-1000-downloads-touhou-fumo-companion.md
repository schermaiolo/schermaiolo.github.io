This is a small milestone, but I am so excited about people using it!

**Touhou Fumo Companion has passed 1,000 combined downloads.**

At the time of writing, that is **880 downloads on Open VSX** and **126 on the Visual Studio Marketplace** — **1,006 in total**.

![Touhou Fumo Companion spin showcase](/assets/images/log/touhou-fumo-companion-spin-showcase.gif)

I know that, in the grand scheme of open-source projects, a thousand downloads is not some enormous number. But for this project, and for me, it feels pretty darn special.

I originally made Touhou Fumo Companion because I was inspired by the pixeled Pokémon pet extension, and was like "Wouldn't be hilarious to have one for FUMOS??".. and so I went into the Touhou rabbit hole once again. And I am commited to updating it normally with at least one new Fumo a month!

Also, an adjustment having some more fun idle frames for all of them, but in the future!

And honestly, I did not really expect many people outside of my own little corner of the internet to install it. But yet, apparently, more than a thousand downloads later, some of you also wanted a fumo staring at your code.

That makes me genuinely happy.

**thank you.**
